# Asset font photo video and music rights

Audit started 18 November 2026; continued 19 November 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Independent local fixture/source evidence only.

The conservative inventory hashes 358 local public/imported media and font files, plus source-discovered external URLs. [Asset ledger](evidence/media-license-asset-inventory.json) includes path, SHA256, size/format, source/author uncertainty, adjacent notices, grant/credit/release/redistribution fields, confidence and owner. It is a superset inventory, not proof every file is reached by every family.

Some precise font directories retain OFL notices (Silk Moul/Kantumruy/Cormorant, selected default/Khmer/Celestial fonts); Floral effects include a Kenney notice. These are positive evidence to match to exact binaries/revisions, not a blanket license for unrelated font copies, SVGs, photos or recordings. Marketing provenance manifests do not certify invitation portraits.

The bundled `Instrumental Wedding Music (VioSounds Cover).m4a` and Khmer MP3 names do not establish recording/composition streaming or SaaS/customer-output grants. Unsplash/Pexels URLs do not prove model/property releases or the acquisition/use scope. Source comments claiming in-house SVG authorship are not independently signed provenance.

Rights status remains unverified wherever the exact grant is absent. Existing artwork/tracks are preserved; no replacement content was authorized. Authored empty media no longer revives demo photos/music in the repaired families. Content/brand owner must match each hash to the author, acquisition record, exact grant, credit/release and SaaS/shared-output scope before commercial release. Phase14 owns asset workflow; Phase26/27 owns the release gate.
