# Chapter 11 — Responsive, Safe Area, Keyboard & Focus

## 1. Responsive Craftsmanship

- Inspected across viewports: 320px, 360px, 390px, 430px, 600px, 768px, 820px, 1024px, 1440px, 1920px, and short landscape 844x390.
- No document-level horizontal overflow at 320px.
- Touch target sizes maintain >= 44x44 CSS px for primary buttons and filter chips.
- Keyboard navigation: Full Tab/Shift+Tab accessibility across sort selects, chips, search inputs, clear buttons, cards, and comparison tables.
