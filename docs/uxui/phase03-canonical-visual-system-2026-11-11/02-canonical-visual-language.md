# Canonical visual language

KoupReng management surfaces use warm cream, paper, forest ink and restrained gold. Cream provides breathing room; white and subtle paper layers define card depth; forest is the main action color; gold marks editorial emphasis rather than every control. Dark mode uses forest near-black and warm legible text. Dense operations remain legible without copying the invitation's decorative art into admin tables.

Hierarchy: a clear page title, one primary action per task area, a secondary explanatory line, then grouped form/table content. Prefer 72ch reading measures, deliberate 16–48px gutters, 44px comfortable controls, 12px control and 20px panel radii. Shadows are low contrast, with borders supplying the principal containment. Use live status words and icons alongside status color; gold never represents payment truth.

Marketing may retain its Silk & Ink editorial typography and decorative gold. Auth and owner use calm forms and workspace cards. Admin uses the same semantic palette at greater information density. Studio chrome is a dark authoring environment with its own neutral tokens; its preview iframe owns the invitation design. Templates retain their distinct art directions. The Phase 03 changes are foundation mapping and selected real-surface adoption, not a universal page redesign.

Light pairs include white on forest `#2e433a`, dark ink on cream `#fbf6ee`, muted `#59615c` on cream, and gold `#926c34` on white. Dark pairs include `#f0f3ee` on `#172019`, muted `#b9c7ba` on `#202a24`, and dark ink on pale forest action `#b7d2b8`. Contrasts are calculated in [08](08-accessibility-contrast-and-visual-qa.md); actual page overrides still require route checks.
