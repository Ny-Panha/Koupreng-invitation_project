# Wedding detail background source

Selected on 2026-11-06 after comparing three free photographic backgrounds.

- Photograph: [White Flowers on White Background](https://www.pexels.com/photo/white-flowers-on-white-background-7965228/).
- Photographer: Eva Bronzini.
- [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) checked on the selection date. It permits use within websites and invitation designs, including commercial use; attribution is optional. The photograph is incorporated into this invitation design, not offered as a separate stock download.
- Original listed dimensions: 5568 x 3712.
- Download: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/7965228/pexels-photo-7965228.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1600`.
- Delivered dimensions: 1600 x 1067; 132,011 bytes; unchanged downloaded JPEG bytes.
- Working source: `assets/khmer-floral-gold/realistic-effects/white-blossom-paper.jpg`.
- Runtime: `apps/frontend-user/public/templates/khmer-floral-gold/effects/realistic/white-blossom-paper.jpg`.

The white blossoms, green foliage and clear paper center suit the ivory/gold
invitation. Welcome/details use a static portrait-oriented backdrop at 28%
opacity, with no blur. The original generated corner branches remain fully opaque.
Data-saving mode omits this optional photograph. The first page retains the
owner-selected white/green border instead. Source and runtime hashes appear in
[the asset ledger](REALISTIC_EFFECTS_ASSETS.json).

The other inspected candidates were Tara Winstead's beige daisy background
(Pexels 7663246) and Cup of Couple's rose/stationery photograph (8015507).
The chosen photograph provides more clear paper space and has no printed text or
stationery rectangle behind the live wedding content.
