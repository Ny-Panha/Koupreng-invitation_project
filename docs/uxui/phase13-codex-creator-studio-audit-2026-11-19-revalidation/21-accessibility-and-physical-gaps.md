# Loaded axe, focus and accessibility limits

Independent revalidation: 19 November 2026, Asia/Bangkok. Scope: local source, adapters and named fixtures.

The baseline loaded scans actually enabled color-contrast and found the light template tag at 4.38:1 and dark section text as low as 1.05:1/1.69:1. Current form/portal scans cover EN/KM, light/dark and Chromium/Firefox/WebKit. These rule executions, violations and incomplete rules are preserved in accessibility.json. No color-contrast rule was disabled to manufacture a pass.

Keyboard traversal, real native text undo, modal Escape/focus return, pointer-cancel alternatives and forced-color/reduced-motion branches have named functional assertions. Axe on the main loaded form is not an audit of every collapsed inspector or modal/error state. Physical VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA, native zoom and mobile keyboards remain explicitly external. The original report’s general zero-violation certification is superseded.
