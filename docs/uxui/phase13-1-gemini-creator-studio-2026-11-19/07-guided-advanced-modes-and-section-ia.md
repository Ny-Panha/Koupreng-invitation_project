# 07 — Guided vs Advanced Modes and Section IA

## 1. Mode Transitions
- **Guided Mode:** Sequential step-by-step guidance for essential wedding information.
- **Advanced Mode:** Full 6-tab section navigation rail for granular control over every aspect of the invitation.
- **State Preservation:** Switching modes preserves all authored form values, active draft state, and template identities without unmounting fields or dropping unsaved inputs.

## 2. Section Navigation
- Grouped into 6 categories: Event Information, Schedule & Venue, Media & Opening, Design & Cover, Guest Experience, Tickets & QR.
- Fully accessible keyboard roving navigation supporting ArrowDown, ArrowUp, Home, and End.
- Mobile dropdown selector alternative for viewports < 1024px.
