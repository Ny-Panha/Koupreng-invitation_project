# Khmer Celestial art direction

Implemented direction: Angkor Royal and Romduol garden, preserving jade, sage, ivory and antique gold and the original photographs, botanical frame and ceremonial identity. Original thin scroll corners suggest carved borders without copying a temple relief. A small six-petal cream/gold emblem uses the distinction between outer and inner petals; it is a Romduol-inspired design study, not a botanical plate.

Two scoped screenshots compare the implemented jade hybrid and an ivory botanical study (`concept-angkor-romduol.png`, `concept-ivory-romduol.png`). The hybrid retains the established template identity and stronger hierarchy. No alternative design-selection state or runtime Canva dependency was added.

APSARA and UNESCO references informed the restraint and architectural context. Figural Apsara/Devata imagery was considered and deliberately deferred: inventing a ritual figure without qualified cultural review would add less value than original non-figural framing. Existing generic roses/jasmine/lotus decorations are not relabeled as Romduol.

Names keep a crisp main glyph layer and shallow highlight. New frames occupy corners and headings, not paragraphs or QR controls. Motion is bounded; no new animation engine or WebGL dependency is introduced. Native Khmer/cultural editorial approval remains a release gate.
