# Rendered evidence and export review

`evidence/after` contains current catalogue captures in Khmer/English at mobile 390px and desktop 1440px. `evidence/before` contains controlled original-HEAD captures of Khmer Celestial family/program and the original map editor, captured from the baseline build before changes. The browser matrix checks 320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 widths across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.

The new catalogue fixture and original Phase 11 fixture intentionally differ. Before/after captures demonstrate hierarchy and behavior; they are not presented as pixel-identical fixtures or a numerical image-diff score. All names, locations, routes and guest messages in new evidence are fictitious. No private map topology, real phone number or guest dump is included.

Manual image inspection covered Khmer Celestial family/program/map, Royal Garden English, Royal Lotus English and Silk Heritage English. Findings and fixes are in report 06. PNG exports of the map and monogram were viewed directly: Khmer glyphs shape correctly, the monogram has transparency, geometry remains clean, and destination labels wrap without colliding with parking. Long labels retain their full textual legend. SVG contains embedded font bytes and OFL metadata (metadata-only trailing whitespace is normalized in the review copies); PNG and WebP signatures/dimensions are checked by real browser downloads in three engines.

Scoped Axe checks cover map canvas/layers, guest locale selector and inherited Phase 8/9/10 controls. Native Chromium CDP tests exercise touch and cancellation; Firefox/WebKit supply pointer/keyboard coverage and explicitly skip CDP injection. This is not physical-device or manual screen-reader evidence. Native Khmer editorial and cultural review remain pending.

The review page and sanitized ZIP provide representative images and reproducible source/test pointers. Raw traces and logs remain ignored local artifacts because they can contain synthetic access credentials. Historical Phase 8–11 screenshot/report outputs regenerated by tests are restored before staging; this release stages its own evidence only.
