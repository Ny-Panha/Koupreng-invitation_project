## 1. Zero External Runtime NPM Bloat Principle
Rather than installing 10 bloated third-party npm packages, Koupreng integrates curated in-tree adaptations in `src/shared/ui/vendor/`:
1. **shadcn/ui** — Accessible dialogs, dropdowns, and button primitives.
2. **Untitled UI** — Clean SaaS pricing cards and empty states.
3. **Magic UI** — BorderBeam and AnimatedGradientText effects.
4. **Kokonut UI** — Toolbar and input group styling.
5. **React Bits** — Text decrypt and spotlight card hover shaders.
6. **Animate UI** — Fluid accordion and drawer physics.
7. **Aceternity UI** — MovingBorder and TracingBeam animations.
8. **Badtz UI** — 3D envelope fold and ribbon badges.
9. **Lightswind UI** — Luminescent gold aura and glowing pill buttons.
10. **Skiper UI** — Skip links and floating bottom navigation sheets.

All implementations strictly adhere to MIT/Apache 2.0 terms, guaranteeing full commercial redistribution rights.
