# Chapter 03 — Original UX Art Direction & Design System

## 1. Aesthetic Philosophy: The Prestigious Cambodian Atelier

Koupreng's wedding invitation marketplace departs from standard SaaS card grids, adopting the character of a fine art atelier. The visual language conveys warmth, cultural reverence, and understated modern luxury.

### Palette Roles
- **Base Canvas:** Ivory / Warm Cream (`#FBF9F5` in light mode; `#0B111A` in dark mode).
- **Ink & Typography:** Deep Forest Ink (`#1A2B23` / `#0D1912` in light mode; `#E6ECE8` in dark mode).
- **Ceremonial Accent:** Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#C59747`, `#AA7C30`).
- **Semantic Badges:** 
  - Emerald Green for Free designs (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#047857`).
  - Warm Gold for Premium designs (`rgba(197, 151, 71, 0.15)`, text `#8E641B`).
  - Slate for Demo Only (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)`, text `#475569`).

### Typography & Khmer Shaping
- **Khmer Heading Multiplier:** Minimum 1.6 line-height to accommodate upper and lower vowel diacritics.
- **Font Pairing:** Authentic Cambodian fonts (`Moul`, `Preahvihear`, `Siemreap`, `Kantumruy Pro`) paired with high-quality Latin editorial serif/sans.
- **No Inappropriate Formatting:** No uppercase transformation or artificial letter-spacing applied to Khmer script.
