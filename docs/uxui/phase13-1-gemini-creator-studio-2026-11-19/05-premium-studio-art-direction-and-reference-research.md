# 05 — Premium Studio Art Direction and Reference Research

## 1. Visual Identity
- **Canvas:** Calm ivory and silk canvas (`#f4f6f5`, `#fffdf8`).
- **Primary Ink:** Deep botanical forest (`#17372d`, `#245740`).
- **Accents:** Restrained antique gold (`#98743b`, `#d9c8a9`).
- **Alerts:** Gentle rose/crimson (`#a22e29`, `#fff7f5`) used strictly for validation and conflict states.
- **Elevation:** Clean dimensional layering with believable natural shadows (`rgba(36, 87, 64, 0.15)`).

## 2. Research & Inspiration
- Figma / Webflow workbench concepts: Sticky command bar, clear document status, separated navigation rail and inspector.
- Notion command clarity: Inline helper text and progressive disclosure.
- Dribbble design-workbench references: Hierarchy above the fold, balanced whitespace.
- All inspirations were translated into original semantic HTML and Tailwind/CSS tokens without copying proprietary assets or code.
