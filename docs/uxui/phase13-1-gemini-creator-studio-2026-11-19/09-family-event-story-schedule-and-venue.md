# 09 — Family, Event, Story, Schedule, and Venue

## 1. Authoring Sections
- **Event Info:** Title, date, time, timezone, and bilingual text presets.
- **Family:** Groom/bride parents, honorifics, and family arrangement layout.
- **Schedule & Venue:** Multi-day agenda, ceremony times, Google Maps links, and sketch map uploads.
- **Story & Guest Experience:** Our story chapters, wedding party members, dress code color swatches, FAQ accordion, and KHQR gift accounts.

## 2. Data Persistence
All fields serialize cleanly into `contentJson` and survive roundtrip save and reload without data loss.
