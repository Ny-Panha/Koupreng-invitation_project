# 19 — EN/KM Cultural Typography and Timezones

## 1. Bilingual Typography
- Khmer fonts (Kantumruy Pro, Moul, Siemreap) paired with Inter and Playfair.
- Generous line height (1.8+) to ensure Khmer diacritics and coeng consonants render without clipping.
- Authentic ceremonial phrasing defaults ("សិរីមង្គលអាពាហ៍ពិពាហ៍", "សូមគោរពអញ្ជើញ").

## 2. Timezone Integrity
- Event dates and reception times preserve wall-clock time in Asia/Phnom_Penh without arbitrary UTC date shifts.
