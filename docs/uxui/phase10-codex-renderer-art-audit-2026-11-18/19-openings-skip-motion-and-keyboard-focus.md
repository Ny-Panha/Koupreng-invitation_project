# Openings skip motion and keyboard focus

Audit started 18 November 2026; continued 19 November 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Independent local fixture/source evidence only.

Opening controls were audited as behavior, not by a Skip text node. DigitalYes keeps a stable bypass while unfolding and removes its purchase bar until the invitation is open; timers are cleaned and reduced preference can interrupt. Emerald uses a native badge, avoids nested double keyboard handling, inerts hidden content and focuses main. Canva respects closed preview and focuses its newly mounted main. Floral/Royal/Celestial expose initial bypass without removing their normal art actions.

The timing ledger distinguishes automation wall time from click-to-next-painted-frame browser observations. It does not establish a universal p95 <50ms guarantee. Normal-motion illustrations, skipped state, reduced motion and applicable covers have separate records. WithJoy/Bliss are coverless; Boilerplate’s public default is already open while preview can start closed.
