# 03 — premium motion principles and design tiers

Koupreng uses motion to clarify response and spatial continuity, while invitation art keeps its own ceremonial identity. Interaction never waits for an entrance effect. Content is readable before enhancement and focus/labels are stable across animation. Server-owned save, payment, RSVP, publication, and delivery outcomes alone determine success UI.

| Tier | Current token / budget | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional | `--ui-duration-press: 120ms`, feedback alias 160ms | Color/opacity/outline; stable hit target and immediate busy state. |
| Spatial | Overlay alias 200ms | Drawer/dialog moves by transform, with inert/focus/scroll behavior independent of the duration. |
| Editorial | Reveal 240ms | Existing page-owned venue and marketing reveals; no generic route overlay or cross-family reveal. |
| Ceremonial | 700ms reference only; family timing stays owned locally | Guest initiates; Skip stays reachable while opening; reduced-motion preference can finish the opening. |
| 2.5D/3D | No global runtime | Named family only after rights, memory, device, and static-fallback gates. |

`--ui-ease-standard`, `--ui-ease-enter`, and `--ui-ease-exit` supply the management vocabulary. Preserve invitation CSS recipes and special timing where art direction demands it. No global shader, autoplay music, cursor follower, scroll-jacking, or indefinitely repeating task animation was added.
