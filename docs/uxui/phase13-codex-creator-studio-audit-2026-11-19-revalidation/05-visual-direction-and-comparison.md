# Workbench visual direction and changed chrome

Independent revalidation: 19 November 2026, Asia/Bangkok. Scope: local source, adapters and named fixtures.

The inherited forest, ivory and restrained gold workbench remains the design direction. This audit changes actual management chrome: readable section text in real dark mode, a darker light-mode template label, less stacked whitespace in short landscape, a fixed 44px landscape command height, and actions that remain available in the keyboard branch. No template artwork, new image, font dependency or motion engine was introduced.

Baseline and final captures use the same fictional event/account fixture, locale and viewport. The baseline 844×390 title input begins at y=429.98, below the viewport; the final manifest records its actual new position and header height. Scroll/edit/Save/preview reachability is asserted, rather than inferring usability from min-height. Selected published screenshots show only management views; full renderer captures remain local because inherited art rights are not newly certified.
