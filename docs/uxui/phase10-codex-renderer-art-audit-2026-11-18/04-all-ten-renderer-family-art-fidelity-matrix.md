# All ten renderer family art fidelity matrix

Audit started 18 November 2026; continued 19 November 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). Independent local fixture/source evidence only.

The design plan is preservation-led: keep the family's existing palette, font stack, photographic ratios and section order. Spend visual change on usable controls and readable text. There is no new universal invitation skin or library installation. Control flow is `family cover → primary ceremonial open / static Skip → that family’s invitation sections → truthful RSVP`. Coverless portal/editorial families remain coverless. Source tokens and actual art take precedence over unsupported Gemini palette descriptions.
